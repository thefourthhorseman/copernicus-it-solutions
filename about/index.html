About Copernicus IT Solutions

Copernicus IT Solutions designs and delivers business-critical software platforms, modernizes legacy systems, and leads technology transformation from strategy through production, with industry expertise in airline and travel technology. Founded in 2017, with a presence in Orlando, Florida, and Sophia Antipolis, France.

What we believe

From the blueprint of your IT landscape to its realisation

We work closely with IT and business leadership to align IT strategy with company goals, adapt methods and organisation to those goals, and build transformation roadmaps that cover the technical, functional, HR and organisational dimensions — for the long-term vision and the next six months alike.

Five foundations

  1. An IT strategy and leadership framework that the organisation actually shares.
  2. A solution design that fits the business, not the vendor.
  3. A transition architecture that keeps the lights on.
  4. A transformation program with clear governance.
  5. Engineering, migration, testing, and production delivery tied to the transformation roadmap.
