About Copernicus IT Solutions

A boutique engineering consultancy in Sophia Antipolis, France, founded in 2017. Small by design, senior by default.

What we believe

From the blueprint of your IT landscape to its realisation

We work closely with IT and business leadership to align IT strategy with company goals, adapt methods and organisation to those goals, and build transformation roadmaps that cover the technical, functional, HR and organisational dimensions — for the long-term vision and the next six months alike.

Five foundations

  1. An IT strategy and leadership framework that the organisation actually shares.
  2. A solution design that fits the business, not the vendor.
  3. A transition architecture that keeps the lights on.
  4. A transformation program with clear governance.
  5. A build-and-deliver execution plan with a senior team behind it.
Leadership

Leadership team

Renaud Arnoux-Prost

Founder — Product Engineering & Advisory

Over thirty years executing complex digital transformation programs, including VP-level product-engineering leadership at Amadeus. End-to-end development oversight of high-availability, mission-critical applications, with deep expertise in e-retail architectures for travel. Renaud bridges business strategy and technical architecture, structures R&D organisations and delivers concrete, robust solutions — equally involved in strategic vision and hands-on execution, with a strong focus on quality, scalability, value creation and R&D team leadership.
