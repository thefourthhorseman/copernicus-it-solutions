Our four Technology Transformation solutions cover this delivery path. Airline and Travel Technology remains a distinct specialist practice.

IT Strategy & Leadership

Technology strategy, stakeholder alignment, and execution.

We work with the CIO and the heads of enterprise architecture and business solutions to establish the long-term IT vision the organization needs, and to turn it into a roadmap that gets funded and delivered.

  • Establish the long-term IT vision that supports your business strategy and operating model.
  • Build consensus across business and IT around that vision and the transformations it requires.
  • Secure executive sponsorship and align teams on priorities and responsibilities.
  • Define the implementation roadmap and the governance structures, decision rights, and escalation paths needed to execute it.

Solution Architecture & Integration

Architecture aligned with your business requirements.

Industries whose core products are being commoditized will differentiate through digital platforms, and established companies have the business knowledge to lead those platforms. Adopting a mainstream solution “because everyone does” often delivers a false economy when it does not fit the business vision. We evaluate build, buy, and integration options against business fit, total cost of ownership, and delivery risk. We design the architecture and integration approach around the business ambition, operational constraints, and the practical path to implementation.

  • Target architecture and solution design for high-availability, low-latency platforms.
  • Technology choices evaluated against business fit, total cost, resilience, and implementation risk.
  • Hybrid integration and legacy modernization patterns.
  • Migration architectures designed to minimize downtime, with rehearsed cutovers and rollback plans.

Program Management

Global leadership for large-scale transformation programs.

  • Define integrated program roadmaps across business, technology, and organizational workstreams, spanning the business units, regions, and sites of large organizations.
  • Build incremental transformation roadmaps that minimize transformation cost and deliver business value to the market early.
  • Lead international, multicultural programs and their governance.
  • Structure and execute legacy transition and decommissioning programs for IBM Transaction Processing Facility (TPF) and other mainframe estates.

Build & Deliver

An integrated delivery process from requirements to production.

Our software design approach maximizes value creation by reducing handoffs, rework, and lead time across the software delivery lifecycle. We carry product specification, software engineering, integration, testing, data migration, and production readiness through one coordinated delivery path.

  • Full-stack delivery: front end, back end and data layer.
  • Engineering automation and standardized delivery practices: repeatable builds, controlled change management, operational excellence.
  • Real-time, high-volume transactional platforms and marketplaces.
  • Migration validation, cutover rehearsals, rollback planning, and transition to operations.

DevOps and DevSecOps

Our approach in three steps.

Led by Christophe Griva, who has delivered middleware and DevOps for ENGIE, EDF, SNCF, CMA CGM, Capgemini and Tier-1 banks. IBM WebSphere, Oracle WebLogic, open-source stacks, containers, and Kubernetes; automated delivery pipelines and observable infrastructure with integrated security controls for regulated environments.

  1. Assess & Architect Map current pipelines, security posture and compliance gaps.
  2. Automate & Harden Implement CI/CD, infrastructure as code and security scanning; codify policies and quality gates.
  3. Operate & Improve Run, monitor, and continuously tune the platform against agreed service-level objectives (SLOs), with contractual service-level agreements (SLAs) where applicable.
